## 2026-07-11 - Pre-compiling frequently invoked Regex patterns
**Learning:** Python's inline `re.compile` (implicitly called via `re.match` and `re.search`) is reasonably fast due to an internal cache. However, when regexes are executed hundreds or thousands of times within rapid networking loops or complex stdout parsing blocks (like reading `networkquality` logs), extracting them to module-level globals entirely eliminates cache lookup and dictionary-hashing overhead inside the execution path.
**Action:** Always identify inline regex evaluations inside frequently called functions (especially loops or stdout parsers) and refactor them to global `re.compile` declarations for maximum efficiency.
